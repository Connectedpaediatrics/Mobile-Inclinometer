Clinical and technical limitations

This is a measurement aid, not a validated medical device. Results depend on phone placement, sensor calibration, starting position, stabilization and how the movement is performed. It does not directly measure joint range of motion unless the chosen placement and protocol isolate that movement. Axial rotation (including cervical, thoracic, lumbar and hip rotation) cannot reliably be measured with gravity-only phone tilt, particularly in the horizontal plane. Use manual entry from an appropriate separate measurement method. Repeat measures and compare them using the same protocol. No patient identification or personal details are collected.

Sensor values are processed on this device. This prototype makes no network requests and session results are not uploaded; they are lost when the page is refreshed unless exported.

Keep this app on your phone

To add this app to your Home Screen: on iPhone open in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android open in Chrome and choose ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app.

Once the app has been opened online, it can load offline. Motion permission may be requested when you start sensors.
